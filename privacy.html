Privacy Policy

Köklerimiz Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Köklerimiz documentary channel (YouTube @koklerimiztarih) to publish the channel's own short videos and texts to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, the Facebook Page Köklerimiz, Threads, X and TikTok, through each platform's official API. It is not offered to other users.

Data we access. Only the access tokens the platforms issue when the channel owner signs in, and basic account information of those accounts (account ID, name, username, avatar, the privacy options TikTok returns for the account). On TikTok the tool uses the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

How data is used. Solely to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts and to show the owner, before each TikTok post, the account the video will be posted to. Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, readable only by that user, and are never shared, sold or sent anywhere else. The tool keeps the IDs and links of its own posts so nothing is posted twice.

What we do not do. The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of anyone else, does not read other people's profiles, messages, followers or contacts, and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking. It has no servers of its own.

Retention and deletion. Data is kept until the owner removes it or revokes access (TikTok: Settings › Security › Manage app permissions; Facebook/Instagram: Apps and Websites; X: Connected apps; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access or deleting the local configuration removes all data the tool holds.

Children. The tool is not directed at children and processes no children's data.

Contact: through the Köklerimiz YouTube channel, youtube.com/@koklerimiztarih.
